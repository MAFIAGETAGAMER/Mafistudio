MafiFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

MafiFlow is a personal finance management application. This Privacy Policy explains how financial records are stored locally and what may happen when users choose to enable optional artificial intelligence (AI) features through external services.

1. Financial information and local storage

MafiFlow lets users enter and manage information such as income, expenses, savings, and budgets. The application is designed to store these records locally on the user's device. Simply entering or saving these records does not send them to the developer.

2. Optional AI features

AI is not required to use MafiFlow. If a user enables AI using their own API key, MafiFlow may transmit prompts and, depending on the feature used, relevant financial information to the selected provider (such as NVIDIA, OpenAI, or another compatible service) to generate responses or personalized suggestions. Providers may process and retain information under their own privacy and retention policies. Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in prompts.

3. Accounts and API keys

MafiFlow does not require users to create a MafiFlow account to access its core features. Users who enable AI may need a separate account with their chosen AI provider to obtain an API key. That account is governed by the provider's terms and policies.

4. Why information is processed

Information transmitted to an AI service is used to deliver the AI feature requested by the user and, where applicable, to provide suggestions based on the information supplied. AI features are optional.

5. Data retention and deletion

Users can remove locally stored financial records by deleting the application's stored data on their device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove these records, subject to the operating system and any backups. Deleting local records does not necessarily delete information previously transmitted to third-party AI providers. Please consult the relevant provider's policies for retention and deletion options.

6. Security

Users should choose trusted AI providers and use encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Younger users

MafiFlow may be used to keep personal financial records. Younger users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information to external AI providers and seek guidance from a parent or guardian where appropriate.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when MafiFlow's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will indicate the latest update.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the developer contact information shown on MafiFlow's official Google Play listing.
